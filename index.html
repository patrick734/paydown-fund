The mission

Forty trillion dollars. One trade at a time.

The national debt grows by billions every day, and most people feel there is nothing they can do about it. The Paydown Fund turns trading into a small, steady donation against it.

The U.S. Treasury accepts donations toward the debt through its official Pay.gov page, “Gifts to Reduce the Debt Held by the Public”. The fund collects its share of the token tax in a public wallet, donates it there, and posts the official receipt.

We are an independent project. We are not part of, or endorsed by, the U.S. government.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is buying $PAYDOWN a donation to the government?

No. Buying the token is a trade with other people. The fund makes the donations to the Treasury from the tax it collects, and it posts each receipt in the ledger.

Is the 3% a federal tax?

No. It is a charge written into the token contract by this project. It has nothing to do with the IRS or any government body.

How can I check that the money reaches the Treasury?

Watch the fund wallet on the block explorer to see what it collects. Every donation in the ledger links to its official Pay.gov receipt and to the transaction that funded it. Money sitting in the wallet has not been donated until its receipt is posted.

Does the U.S. government endorse this?

No. The Paydown Fund is independent. The Treasury accepts donations toward the debt from the public through Pay.gov, and that is the only connection.

Is this an investment?

No. $PAYDOWN is a community token. Its price can fall to zero, and the tax means each buy and sell costs 3%. Only spend what you are happy to lose.
